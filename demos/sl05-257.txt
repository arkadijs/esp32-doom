Another map with a below-3-minute barrier goal that I 
eventually reached in 1st exit. Route is basically a 
game of connect the dots again, but there's a pacifist 
element to it. When to make noise and wake everything 
up is actually not that simple to answer, the most 
ideal would be doing so immediately so stuff teleports 
in and infights, but this also wakes up arachnotrons 
which almost certainly block the intended path. Vile 
released from the exit is free to revive infighting 
victims anyway so I settled with making noise right 
after passing those spiders. Map is bad for max because 
getting rl quickly is all about luck, the jump for plasma 
gun requires quite a lot of precision, the platform with 
rl fails to lover around 2/3 of time due to extremely 
likely linedef skip, berserk secret can fail to register 
very easily, the only armor is unreachable until the very 
end if you disregard those few armor bonuses and I also 
noticed one trap can be cheesed with sr50 which doesn't
always work. Not sure how fast can this go, but I'm not 
about to find out. Maybe if somebody lowers the time.
